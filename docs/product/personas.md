# Personas

## Persona 1: Ana, the organizer (primary)

- 34 years old, works full time, lives with a partner and one child.
- Buys groceries weekly for the whole household.
- Pain: forgets what is already in the pantry, lets food expire, buys
  duplicates.
- Goals: keep a realistic shopping list, reduce food waste, never run out of
  basics.
- Uses: web app on laptop for planning, phone while shopping.
- Technical comfort: medium.

## Persona 2: Rafael, the shared household member

- 29 years old, shares an apartment with two friends.
- Not the person who plans purchases, but consumes from the stock.
- Pain: does not know what belongs to the household vs. to a roommate.
- Goals: see what is available, add items to the shared list without friction.
- Uses: phone most of the time.
- Technical comfort: high.

## Persona 3: Dona Marta, the careful consumer

- 58 years old, lives alone, cooks daily.
- Pain: worries about expiration dates and safety of stored food.
- Goals: clear alerts about items about to spoil, minimal interface.
- Uses: desktop.
- Technical comfort: low. Needs plain language and large controls.
